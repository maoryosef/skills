---
name: checking-tool-claims
description: Use when you are about to recommend or compare tools, services or a way of using one, or state what they can do, their limits, ratios or costs.
---
Before you state what a tool, database or service supports, or a limit, ratio or cost of it, check its current official docs or measure it, and cite the source next to the claim.
Mark any claim you did not check in this session as unverified, and say what doc or measurement would settle it.
