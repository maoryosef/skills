---
name: keeping-a-doc-in-sync
description: Use when you discuss, decide or change content of a design document or report that is open in the session.
---
End each reply that adds a fact, decision or option to the discussion by saying whether it is in the document yet, and offer to add it.
Keep a running list of what was discussed but is not yet in the local copy or the published copy, and show it with that offer.
Before you report a change to the document as done, search the whole document for every other place it touches, including figures built from a changed number, review findings and open questions.
In that report, name the section or slide of each change in page order, say what you checked, and list anything you left as it was, with the reason.
Before you show the user a preview of the changed document, follow the preview step of the skill that builds it, such as step 5 of accomplish-report, and look at the page yourself first.
When you ask the user to decide on an item, propose a change to the document, or copy a design from another session or document into it, say in one line what each item is, where its requirement came from, and whether it only rewords something already decided. Ask before you write in a requirement the user never stated.
